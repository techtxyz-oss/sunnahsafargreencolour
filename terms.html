1. Hamari service

Shaadi Sunnah ek online introduction/matchmaking platform hai jahan Muslim families aur individuals Nikah ke maqsad se profile bana kar ek dusre ko dhoondh sakte hain. Hum rishta tay karne wale ya shaadi karwane wale agent nahi hain.

2. Eligibility

  • Aapki umr kam se kam 18 saal ho aur aap Nikah ki kanooni umr poori karte hon.
  • Aap sirf Nikah ki serious niyyat se profile banayein — dosti, dating ya time-pass ke liye nahi.
  • Aap kanoon ke mutabiq contract karne ke qabil hon.

3. Account aur sahi jaankari

  • Registration me di gayi sab jaankari sahi, poori aur apni honi chahiye (naam, umr, marital status, education, income, photo).
  • Ek insaan ka ek hi account. Doosre ki identity ya photo use karna mana hai.
  • Apna password surakshit rakhna aapki zimmedari hai. Aapke account se hui activity ke aap zimmedar hain.

4. Kya allowed hai aur kya nahi

Mana hai:

  • Fake, duplicate ya galat profile banana; kisi aur ki photo/details lagana.
  • Kisi ko pareshan karna, dhamkana, gandi ya aapatti-janak baat karna, ya na-mehram ko galat tareeke se contact karna.
  • Paise, gifts, loan, “registration fee”, OTP, bank/UPI details maangna ya kisi ko thagne ki koshish karna.
  • Kisi ki jaankari, photo ya number bina ijazat share ya screenshot karke failana.
  • Spam, advertising, dusri website/service ka promotion, ya bulk/automated (bot, scraper) use.
  • Adult, nafrat failane wali, violent ya kanoon-virodhi content.
  • Website ki security todne ya dusron ke accounts tak pahunchne ki koshish.

Allowed aur protsahit: family/wali ki ijazat se baat karna, sach batana, aur izzat ke saath pesh aana.

Har naya profile admin review ke baad hi live hota hai. Hum bina pehle bataye kisi bhi profile, photo ya message ko reject, edit, hide, suspend ya delete kar sakte hain agar wo in Terms, Community Guidelines ya kanoon ke khilaf lage. Report kiye gaye profiles ki jaanch ki jati hai. Hamara faisla antim hoga.
