Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · shaadisunnah.com

Shaadi Sunnah (“we”, “us”) shaadisunnah.com chalata hai — ek Muslim matrimony platform jo Nikah ke liye families ko introduce karta hai. Ye Privacy Policy batati hai ki hum kaun si jaankari lete hain, kyun lete hain, kaise surakshit rakhte hain, aur aapke paas kya adhikaar hain. Website use karke aap is policy se sehmat hote hain.

1. Jaankari jo hum collect karte hain

  • Account: email address aur password (password hashed form me authentication service sambhalti hai).
  • Profile: naam, gender, date of birth, maslak/sect, mother tongue, profession, education, marital status, height, native place, annual income, bio/about, aur profile photo (optional).
  • Contact & family details: mobile number, WhatsApp number, wali/guardian ka naam aur number.
  • Usage: shortlist, bheje/mile hue interests, reports, blocks, notifications, aur basic technical data (IP address, browser/device type) jo servers ke logs me aa sakta hai.
  • Careers / enquiries: naam, phone, city, role, experience, link aur message jo aap Careers form ya WhatsApp/call par dete hain.

2. Hum jaankari kaise use karte hain

  • Aapka account banane aur profile dikhane ke liye.
  • Profiles review/approve karne aur fake ya galat profiles hatane ke liye.
  • Matches suggest karne aur mutual interest ke baad contact share karne ke liye.
  • Reports, fraud aur misuse ki jaanch ke liye, aur platform ko safe rakhne ke liye.
  • Aapke sawalon ka jawab dene aur zaruri notifications bhejne ke liye.
  • Kanooni zimmedariyan nibhane ke liye.

Hum aapka personal data bechte nahi hain aur advertisers ko nahi dete.

3. Kaun kya dekh sakta hai

  • Logged-out visitors ko photos aur full naam blur dikhte hain.
  • Aapka mobile aur WhatsApp number profile card par kabhi public nahi hota; ye tabhi dikhta hai jab dono taraf se interest accept ho.
  • Platform admins profile review, moderation aur safety ke liye profile aur contact details dekh sakte hain.
  • Jo jaankari aap profile me bharte hain (jaise age, education, profession, city) wo approved profile par dusre registered users ko dikh sakti hai.
Dhyan rahe: jab aap kisi se mutual interest ke baad number share karte hain, uske baad wo baat-cheet hamare control me nahi rehti. Apni family ke saath hi aage badhein.

4. Third-party services

Website chalane ke liye hum bharosemand service providers use karte hain, jo aapka data hamari taraf se process karte hain:

  • Supabase — database, login/authentication aur photo storage.
  • Google Fonts, Font Awesome (cdnjs/Cloudflare), jsDelivr, Tailwind CDN — fonts, icons aur scripts load karne ke liye (inhe aapka IP address mil sakta hai).
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — jab aap WhatsApp button par click karke humse chat karte hain, wo baat-cheet WhatsApp ki apni privacy policy ke tahat hoti hai.

5. Cookies aur browser storage

Hum ads wali tracking cookies use nahi karte. Login session banaye rakhne ke liye aur first-visit welcome screen yaad rakhne ke liye aapke browser ka local storage use hota hai. Aap browser settings se ise clear kar sakte hain; uske baad dobara login karna pad sakta hai.

6. Data kitne time tak rakha jata hai

Jab tak aapka account active hai tab tak. Account delete karne ki request par hum profile aur contact details kaafi had tak hata dete hain; kuch records (jaise fraud/report logs ya kanooni zarurat) zaruri avadhi tak rakhe ja sakte hain. Careers applications zarurat poori hone ke baad hata di jati hain.

7. Aapke adhikaar

  • Apni jaankari dekhne aur My Profile se sudharne ka.
  • Profile ya account delete karwane ka.
  • Consent wapas lene ka, aur data ke use par aapatti uthane ka.
  • Shikayat ya sawal ke liye grievance contact se sampark karne ka.

Request karne ke liye neeche diye number par WhatsApp ya call karein. Hum pehchan confirm karne ke baad request poori karenge.

8. Security

Hum HTTPS, access controls aur admin-only functions jaise upay use karte hain. Koi bhi online system 100% surakshit nahi hota, isliye apna password kisi se share na karein aur strong password rakhein. Kisi bhi suspicious activity ki turant report karein.

9. Age

Ye service sirf 18 saal ya usse zyada umr ke logon ke liye hai, aur Nikah ke liye kanooni umr (India me aam taur par ladke ke liye 21, ladki ke liye 18 saal) poori honi chahiye. Parents/guardians apne bachon ki taraf se sirf kanooni umr hone par hi profile bana sakte hain. Agar hume pata chale ki koi underage hai, to profile hata diya jayega.

10. Is policy me badlav

Hum time-time par ye policy update kar sakte hain. Naya version isi page par “Last updated” date ke saath dikhega.
