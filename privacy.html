Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

ShaadiSunnah (also called Sunnah Safar) is a Muslim matrimony platform available at shaadisunnah.com. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, and what choices you have. By creating an account or using the site you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the site.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: email address and password (passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not visible to us in readable form).
  • Profile data: name, gender, date of birth, sect, mother tongue, state, education, profession, income (optional), bio, prayer habit, family details, guardian (wali) details and photo.
  • Private contact data: mobile number, WhatsApp number, pincode and guardian contact. These are kept in a separate private area and are not shown on your profile card.
  • Activity data: interests sent and received, shortlists, reports, blocks and notifications.
  • Career applications: name, phone, city, role, experience, optional link and message, if you apply through our Careers page.
  • Technical data: basic device, browser and network information that our hosting and infrastructure providers process to deliver and secure the site.
  • On your device: your login session, your language choice and (if you use the Biodata Maker) a draft of your biodata are stored in your browser. The Biodata Maker works in your browser and does not upload your details to our server.

3. Why we use your data

  • To create and run your account and show your profile to other members.
  • To review profiles before they go live and to keep the community safe (fake profiles, harassment, fraud).
  • To show match information (for example, same state or sect) between you and another member.
  • To process interests, notifications and your reports or blocks.
  • To respond to support requests and career applications.
  • To meet legal obligations and to enforce our Terms of Service.

We process your data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you register and which you can withdraw at any time (see section 8), and where the law requires or permits it.

4. Who can see your data

  • Visitors who are not logged in see blurred photos and names. Contact details are never shown on profile cards.
  • Logged-in members can see your profile details. Your mobile and WhatsApp numbers are shared with another member only after an interest between you is accepted.
  • Our admin team can view profile data to review and moderate profiles. For safety or verification purposes an admin may view your contact details. Each such access is recorded in an internal audit log with a reason.
  • Service providers who help us run the site, such as our database, authentication and file-storage provider (Supabase), our hosting provider (GitHub Pages), our DNS and email-routing provider (Cloudflare), and font and script delivery networks. They process data only to provide their service.
  • Authorities when we are legally required to share data, or to protect the safety of our members.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not show advertisements. If we add analytics or advertising tools in future, we will update this policy first.

5. Where data is stored

Your data is stored with our infrastructure providers and may be processed on servers located outside India. We choose providers that use industry-standard security practices.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your profile, photo and private contact details within a reasonable period, except for records we must keep to meet legal obligations or to handle abuse reports and disputes. Career applications are kept only as long as needed to consider your application and for a reasonable period after.

7. Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted database permissions to protect your data. No system is completely secure, so please also protect your password and do not share your login with anyone. If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as required by law.

8. Your rights and choices

Under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may:

  • ask to access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct or update your data (most of it can be edited in "My Profile");
  • ask us to erase your data or delete your account;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect processing done before withdrawal);
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity; and
  • make a complaint to us, and if unresolved, to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, write to support@shaadisunnah.com or message us on WhatsApp. We may need to verify that the request really comes from you.

9. Children

ShaadiSunnah is only for people who are 18 or older and legally eligible to marry. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we will remove it. Parents and guardians may contact us to request removal.
