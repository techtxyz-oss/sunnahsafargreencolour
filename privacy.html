Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · shaadisunnah.com

Short mein: Aapki jaankari bechi nahi jaati. Mobile/WhatsApp number tabhi dikhta hai jab dono taraf se interest accept ho. Aap apna data dekh, sudhaar aur hatwa sakte hain.

1. Hum kaun hain

ShaadiSunnah (“Sunnah Safar”, “hum”, “hamara”) shaadisunnah.com par chalne wala ek Muslim matrimony platform hai, jo Nikah ke liye munasib rishta dhoondhne mein madad karta hai. Ye policy batati hai ki hum aapki kaunsi jaankari lete hain, kyun lete hain, aur use kaise surakshit rakhte hain.

2. Hum kaunsi jaankari lete hain

  • Account: email aur password (password hashed rehta hai, hum use dekh nahi sakte).
  • Profile: naam, gender, janm tithi (age), mazhab/maslak (sect), matrimonial bhasha (mother tongue), shehar, native place, shiksha, profession, income (optional), height, marital status, parivaar ki jaankari, namaz ki aadat, bio aur profile photo.
  • Contact: mobile number, WhatsApp number, pincode (optional), wali ka naam aur number.
  • Activity: interest bhejna/accept/decline, shortlist, block, report, referral code.
  • Technical: basic device/browser jaankari aur logs jo site chalane aur surakshit rakhne ke liye zaroori hain.

Hum aapse religion ke alawa koi aisi jaankari nahi maangte jo rishta dhoondhne ke liye zaroori na ho (jaise bank, Aadhaar ya PAN details).

3. Hum jaankari ka istemal kyun karte hain

  • Aapka account banane aur profile ko review/approve karne ke liye.
  • Search, filters aur matches dikhane ke liye.
  • Interest bhejne-receive karne aur, dono taraf se accept hone par, contact share karne ke liye.
  • Fake, galat ya ghalat istemal wali profiles ko rokne, reports/blocks sambhalne aur platform ko surakshit rakhne ke liye.
  • Aapko zaroori notifications aur support dene ke liye.

4. Aapki jaankari kaun dekh sakta hai

  • Visitors / bina login ke: sirf approved profiles ki basic jaankari. Mobile aur WhatsApp number kabhi profile card par nahi dikhte.
  • Contact details (mobile, WhatsApp, wali): sirf tab dusre member ko dikhte hain jab aapne unka interest accept kiya ho (ya aapka interest unhone accept kiya ho).
  • Hamari admin team: profile review, reports aur support ke liye zaroorat padne par.
  • Profile photo: aapki upload ki hui photo hamare storage mein rakhi jaati hai. Photo/naam ko logged-out visitors ke liye dhundhla (blur) dikhane ka intezaam hai; kripya aisi photo hi daalein jise aap platform par dikhane mein comfortable hon.

Hum aapki jaankari bechte nahi aur vigyapan (ads) ke liye kisi ko nahi dete.

5. Service providers (jinke through site chalti hai)

Site chalane ke liye hum bharosemand technology providers istemal karte hain, jaise Supabase (login, database aur photo storage), Google Fonts aur CDN services (fonts/icons/scripts ke liye). Ye sirf utni jaankari process karte hain jitni service dene ke liye zaroori hai. Inke servers India ke bahar bhi ho sakte hain.

6. Kitne samay tak rakhte hain

Jab tak aapka account active hai, hum aapki jaankari rakhte hain. Account/profile delete karne ka anurodh karne par hum use hata dete hain; kanoon ya fraud-rokne ke liye zaroori hone par thodi jaankari (jaise report records) kam samay ke liye rakhi ja sakti hai.

7. Aapke adhikaar

Aap kabhi bhi: apni jaankari dekh/sudhaar sakte hain (My Profile se), apna profile hide/delete karne ya data hatane ka anurodh kar sakte hain, consent wapas le sakte hain, aur shikayat kar sakte hain. Iske liye neeche diye gaye contact par likhein. Hum aapka anurodh uchit samay ke andar dekhenge. Ye policy India ke Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 aur Information Technology Act, 2000 ki bhavna ke anusar likhi gayi hai.

8. Suraksha

Hum jaankari ko surakshit rakhne ke liye encrypted connection (HTTPS), access controls aur admin review jaise upay istemal karte hain. Phir bhi internet par koi system 100% surakshit nahi hota; kripya apna password gopniya rakhein aur kisi ko na batayein.

9. 18 saal se kam umar

ShaadiSunnah sirf 18+ ya usse upar ke logon ke liye hai (aur wo jo apne desh ke kanoon ke mutabiq shaadi ke yogya hon). Agar hamein pata chale ki koi nabalig profile bana hai, hum use turant hata denge.

10. Cookies aur local storage

Hum login session aur choti suvidhaon (jaise pehli baar ka Bismillah screen dobara na dikhana) ke liye browser storage/cookies istemal karte hain. Hum tracking ya vigyapan wali cookies nahi lagate. Aap browser settings se inhe band kar sakte hain, par login kaam na kare to isse samasya ho sakti hai.

11. Badlav

Hum ye policy samay-samay par badal sakte hain. Badlav is page par “Last updated” tarikh ke saath dikhenge; zaroori badlav hone par hum aapko batane ki koshish karenge.

12. Shikayat / Grievance

Privacy ya data se judi koi bhi shikayat ya sawal ho to neeche diye gaye support contact par likhein. Hum use dekhkar jawab denge.
