    Quran & Hadith on Nikah

    Qur'an — Surah An-Nisa 4:1

    يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ اتَّقُوا رَبَّكُمُ الَّذِي خَلَقَكُم مِّن نَّفْسٍ وَاحِدَةٍ وَخَلَقَ مِنْهَا زَوْجَهَا

    "O mankind, be mindful of your Lord, who created you from a single soul and created from it its mate."

    "ऐ लोगो! अपने रब से डरो, जिसने तुम्हें एक जान से पैदा किया, और उसी जान से उसकी जोड़ी (पत्नी) को पैदा किया।"

    💡 Husband aur wife ek hi insaan ki do shaakhein hain. Dono ek doosre ke bina adhure hain — yahi islami rishte ki bunyad hai.

    Qur'an — Surah Al-Baqarah 2:187

    هُنَّ لِبَاسٌ لَّكُمْ وَأَنتُمْ لِبَاسٌ لَّهُنَّ

    "They are a garment for you, and you are a garment for them."

    "वे तुम्हारे लिए वस्त्र हैं और तुम उनके लिए वस्त्र हो।"

    💡 Kya khoobsurat misaal hai! Jaise libaas badan ko dhakta, izzat karta, thand se bachata hai — waise hi shohar aur biwi ek doosre ke liye hain. Ek doosre ki hifazat, izzat aur parda.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Find answers to common questions.

    How does profile review work?+

    Every new profile stays pending until our admin team reviews and approves it. Only approved profiles appear in search.

    Who can see my photo and details?+

    Photos and full names are blurred for visitors who are not logged in. Contact details are never shown on your profile card.

    Can I hide my mobile number?+

    Yes. Your mobile and WhatsApp numbers are shared only after an interest is accepted.

    What happens after mutual interest?+

    Once an interest is accepted, contact details become visible so you and your families can talk further.

    What if someone misbehaves?+

    Use Report or Block on their profile. Our team reviews every report.
