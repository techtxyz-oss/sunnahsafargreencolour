    Quran & Hadith on Nikah

    Qur'an — Surah An-Nisa 4:1

    يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ اتَّقُوا رَبَّكُمُ الَّذِي خَلَقَكُم مِّن نَّفْسٍ وَاحِدَةٍ وَخَلَقَ مِنْهَا زَوْجَهَا

    "Ae logo! Apne Rabb se daro jisne tumhe ek jaan se paida kiya, aur usi jaan se uski zoj (biwi) ko paida kiya."

    💡 Husband aur wife ek hi insaan ki do shaakhein hain. Dono ek doosre ke bina adhure hain — yahi islami rishte ki bunyad hai.

    Qur'an — Surah Al-Baqarah 2:187

    هُنَّ لِبَاسٌ لَّكُمْ وَأَنتُمْ لِبَاسٌ لَّهُنَّ

    "Woh tumhara libaas hain aur tum unka libaas ho."

    💡 Kya khoobsurat misaal hai! Jaise libaas badan ko dhakta, izzat karta, thand se bachata hai — waise hi shohar aur biwi ek doosre ke liye hain. Ek doosre ki hifazat, izzat aur parda.

    Hadith

    "Nikah is my Sunnah. Whoever turns away from my Sunnah is not of me."

    "निकाह मेरी सुन्नत है। जो मेरी सुन्नत से मुँह मोड़े, वह मुझसे नहीं।"

    — Sahih Muslim

    Hadith

    "The best of you are those who are best to their wives, and I am the best among you to my wives."

    "तुम में सबसे अच्छा वह है जो अपनी पत्नियों के साथ सबसे अच्छा व्यवहार करे।"

    — Sunan al-Tirmidhi
