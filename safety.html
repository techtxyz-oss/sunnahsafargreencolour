Community Guidelines & Safety

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · shaadisunnah.com

Shaadi Sunnah ka maqsad hai ki Nikah ka safar izzat, sach aur privacy ke saath ho. Ye guidelines har member par lagu hoti hain.

Contents: Community rulesSafety tipsScam se bacheinReport kaise kareinHum kya action lete hain

1. Community rules

  • Sirf Nikah ki niyyat se judein.
  • Sahi jaankari aur apni asli photo (agar lagate hain) hi dein.
  • Family/wali ko shamil rakhein; Islamic aadaab ka khayal rakhein.
  • Kisi ki photo, number ya biodata bina ijazat share na karein.
  • Gaali-galoch, dhamki, pareshani, ya asabhya baat bilkul mana hai.
  • Kisi se paise ya gift maangna ya dena mana hai.

2. Families ke liye safety tips

  • Pehli baat-cheet family/wali ke saath karein, akele nahi.
  • Mulaqat public aur mehfooz jagah par karein, aur kisi bharosemand insaan ko bata kar jayein.
  • Naukri, education, ghar ka pata aur marital status ki independent verification zaroor karein (rishtedaar, mohalla, workplace).
  • Shuruaat me ghar ka poora pata, ID, bank/Aadhaar details share na karein.
  • Jaldbaazi na karein; istikhara aur mashwara karein.

3. Scam se bachein

  • Jo koi paisa, advance, “processing fee”, gift, loan ya emergency help maange, wo scam ho sakta hai.
  • OTP, password ya UPI PIN kisi ko na dein. Shaadi Sunnah team ye kabhi nahi maangti.
  • Foreign/NRI profile jo jaldi bharose me le aur paise ki baat kare, usse sawdhan rahein.
  • Bahut jaldi shaadi ka dabav daalne wale se sawdhan rahein.

4. Kisi profile ko report kaise karein

  1. Profile kholein aur Report button dabayein (ya Block karein).
  2. Ya seedha humein WhatsApp/call karein aur profile ka naam/ID aur wajah batayein.
  3. Screenshot ya chat ka saboot saath dein (agar ho).

Hum report ko 24 ghante me acknowledge karne ki koshish karte hain.

5. Hum kya action lete hain

  • Profile ki jaanch aur zarurat par warning.
  • Galat ya fake profile ko suspend/delete karna.
  • Serious fraud ya threat ki halat me user ko permanently ban karna.
  • Zarurat par kanooni agencies ko jaankari dena. Aap bhi kisi dhokhe ki halat me cybercrime.gov.in ya helpline 1930 par report kar sakte hain.
