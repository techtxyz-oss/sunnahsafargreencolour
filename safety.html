Community Guidelines & Safety

Last updated: 8 October 2026 · shaadisunnah.com

Shaadi Sunnah exists so that the journey to Nikah is carried out with dignity, honesty and privacy. These guidelines apply to every member.

Contents: Community rulesSafety tipsAvoiding scamsHow to reportWhat we do

1. Community rules

  • Join only with a sincere intention of Nikah.
  • Give accurate information and use your own genuine photo (if you add one).
  • Involve your family or wali, and observe Islamic etiquette.
  • Never share anyone’s photo, number or biodata without their permission.
  • Abuse, threats, harassment and indecent language are strictly not allowed.
  • Asking for or giving money or gifts is not allowed.

2. Safety tips for families

  • Hold the first conversations with the family or wali, not alone.
  • Meet in a public, safe place, and tell someone you trust before you go.
  • Independently verify job, education, home address and marital status (through relatives, the neighbourhood and the workplace).
  • Do not share your full home address, ID, bank or Aadhaar details early on.
  • Do not rush. Make istikhara and take advice.

3. Avoiding scams

  • Anyone who asks for money, an advance, a “processing fee”, gifts, a loan or emergency help may be a scammer.
  • Never give your OTP, password or UPI PIN to anyone. The Shaadi Sunnah team will never ask for them.
  • Be careful with overseas or NRI profiles that gain your trust quickly and then bring up money.
  • Be cautious of anyone who pressures you into a very quick marriage.

4. How to report a profile

  1. Open the profile and tap Report (or Block).
  2. Or contact us directly by email or WhatsApp with the profile name or ID and the reason.
  3. Include a screenshot or chat evidence if you have it.

We aim to acknowledge every report within 24 hours.

5. What we do

  • Review the profile and, where needed, issue a warning.
  • Suspend or delete false or fake profiles.
  • Permanently ban users in cases of serious fraud or threats.
  • Share information with law-enforcement agencies where required. If you are cheated, you can also report it at cybercrime.gov.in or call the helpline 1930.
