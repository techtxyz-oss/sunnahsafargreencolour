About ShaadiSunnah

ShaadiSunnah is a Muslim matrimonial platform that helps Muslims find suitable marriage proposals in a simple and respectful way. We encourage honesty, family involvement and serious intentions towards marriage. Operated by Abu Abdurrhman.

By creating a profile or using this site, you agree to the points below. You must be 18 or older and legally eligible to marry.

Your Privacy

  • We collect what is needed to create and run your profile: your name, age, location, education, profession, preferences, photo, contact and wali details, and anything else you choose to add.
  • Approved profiles can be seen by other members and visitors of the site, so share only what you are comfortable with.
  • Your phone/WhatsApp number is not shown on your profile. It becomes visible to another member only after an interest is accepted.

Your Profile

Your Safety Matters

We take reasonable steps to keep the platform safe, but we cannot personally verify every member or guarantee what they share. So please be careful too:

  • Verify important information yourself.
  • Involve your family or wali where appropriate.
  • Do not share sensitive personal or financial information unnecessarily.

  • Never send money to someone you met through ShaadiSunnah, and do not demand or agree to dowry.

Your safety is a shared responsibility. ShaadiSunnah only helps people connect; we are not responsible for what members say or do, or for the outcome of any proposal.
